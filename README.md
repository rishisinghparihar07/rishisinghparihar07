## Hi, I'm Rishi Singh Parihar

I build the tools my operations team runs on.

I'm an **Assistant Manager, Distribution & Operations at Atica Global** in Pune, where I manage a team of seven handling sales operations for US hotels. Alongside running the team, I build the automations, internal tools and dashboards that take manual work off their plate.

**What I've built**

- **Email RFP alert automation** (n8n, Salesforce, Slack). Sorts inbound client emails, creates the Salesforce task and alerts the right associate. Live since Oct 2025 for 20+ associates; missed-email escalations fell from about 30% to 2–5%.
- **Lead generation and contact enrichment** (n8n, Hyperbrowser, Appsmith, Google Maps API). An Appsmith app finds businesses near a hotel by radius and industry; an n8n backend crawls their websites and collects public contact details. In production since Feb 2026.
- **SARAH, automated RFP submission** (core team, Operations). Cross-functional n8n product that finds and submits HotelPlanner RFPs; I wrote the BRDs and mapped the process. Live on 200+ properties, turnaround down from 24+ hours to 2–4 hours.
- **Group Pricing Calculator** (Excel). Recommends group rates from five years of hotel data; 90%+ rate accuracy in the pilot, then handed to the Tech team in Apr 2026.
- **Channel activation automation** (n8n, AI-assisted drafting). One form sends onboarding emails to up to 10 booking channels and tracks every follow-up. Cut activation from 10–15 days to 2–3 days.
- **Operations dashboards** (SQL, Metabase). SLA, task management and exception tracking for the Operations team.
